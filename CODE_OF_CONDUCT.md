# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

PulseLinux is committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Encouraged:**
- Respectful, constructive technical disagreement
- Assuming good intent from other contributors
- Giving and receiving specific, actionable feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

**Not tolerated:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Dismissing contributions based on identity rather than their technical merit
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, the project mailing list, and any official events).

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to conduct@pulselinux.org.
All complaints will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviours they deem
inappropriate.
